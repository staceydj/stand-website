What your support does

Your support pays for the people and systems that deliver the work.

Ambassadors on active duty

Stipends for trained community ambassadors who support survivors on the ground and help them follow their cases through every service.

Data to log and follow cases

Mobile data so ambassadors can log and follow cases.

Case tracing

Day-to-day follow-up of every case through each handover, across police, health, social services and the courts.

Training

Training sessions for ambassadors, police and service providers.

Community awareness

Community information on how to get help and how to use the system.

Evidence for scale

Independent analysis of case outcomes and handover gaps, building the evidence to take the model into new districts across South Africa and the Global South.
