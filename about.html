About STAND

A specialist technical and implementation partner.

We are a women-led technical and implementation partner working where justice, health, protection, and implementation meet.

Who we are

STAND (Social Transformation Action Defined) is a South African women-led nonprofit, a registered Public Benefit Organisation with Section 18A tax-exemption status, and a specialist technical platform working at the intersection of gender-responsive justice, gender-based violence, substance-related harms, public health, and systems accountability.

Founded in 2018 and based in Cape Town, STAND operates as a lean, technically deep organisation, mobilising specialist consultants and collaborators according to programme scope and need. This model keeps STAND agile and responsive while holding the analytical and technical standards of a senior institutional partner.

We are not a generalist development organisation. Our work is intersectional by design, sitting where gender-based violence, substance-related harms, justice and health meet, and we bring the expertise to close the gap between what the evidence shows and what actually gets done.

Four things distinguish how we work

01

We work in the hard middle ground.

Policy commitments exist. Services are theoretically available. Institutions are willing. And yet systems fail. STAND operates in exactly the space where this happens, with the evidence, relationships, and implementation experience to address it.

02

Our credibility is earned, not claimed.

STAND's positioning rests on documented work: assessments completed, training delivered, referral pathways designed, tools produced, coordination systems strengthened. We do not inflate. Every capacity we offer has been demonstrated.

03

We design for how systems actually work.

Our outputs are shaped by operational reality, not only by what policy prescribes or theory recommends. When STAND designs a coordination mechanism or a training curriculum, it is built for the institutions, personnel, and conditions that will actually use it.

04

We combine technical depth with institutional fluency.

STAND is equally credible in a donor briefing room, a law-enforcement training environment, a municipal coordination meeting, and a community-based assessment. That range, held without compromising substance, is what makes STAND a trusted partner across sectors.

Where we started. Where we are now.

STAND began in 2018 in Cape Town, emerging from frontline community engagement and a clear-sighted recognition that the systems meant to protect the most vulnerable people were systematically failing them.

Over time, STAND evolved from community-rooted practice into a highly credible implementation and systems-strengthening partner with growing regional and international relevance. Its earlier community-based treatment work was run in research partnership with the South African Medical Research Council. The work deepened. The evidence base expanded. The institutional relationships strengthened.

Today STAND holds a distinctive position: the credibility of a senior technical partner with the agility of a specialist, senior-led organisation. It is led by Stacey Doorly-Jones, a strategist, qualitative researcher, technical writer, and systems adviser whose career has been built at the intersection of gender justice, substance-related harms, and institutional accountability.

Meet the founder

Formally constituted. Accountable by design.

STAND is registered as a South African Non-Profit Company (NPC) and Public Benefit Organisation (PBO No. 930063426) with Section 18A tax-exemption status, and holds Level 1 B-BBEE status.

The organisation maintains formal governance structures including a Code of Conduct, financial accountability systems, and a commitment to transparent reporting. STAND is governed by a Board of Directors that provides independent, expert-led oversight. Donors and partners can expect the institutional rigour that serious organisations require.

What we stand by

Integrity

We say what we can support and support what we say. Every claim is defensible. Every promise is kept.

Accountability

We track what we commit to, report honestly on what we achieve, and own responsibility for quality and governance.

Dignity

The people our work is meant to serve remain visible and respected throughout. No pity language. No saviour framing.

Rigour

Technical quality is non-negotiable. We distinguish between what has been done, what we are positioned to do, and what we propose.
