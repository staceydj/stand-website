# Stacey's voice (personal and campaign messages)

Set by Stacey on 26 November 2026. Use this for every message written in her name.

- Warm, sincere and reflective. Flowing paragraphs, not bullet lists or slogans.
- Opens with a personal line ("I'm asking a small favour from the people closest to me").
- Says why it is close to her heart: this is not a campaign made for 16 Days, it is the work STAND does every day.
- Names the human reality plainly: "Behind every case is a real woman who has already had to find the courage to report what happened to her. She should not then have to fight the system simply to find out where her case went."
- Explains the system simply: it follows a case through the handovers between police, health, social services, prosecution and the courts.
- Asks for sharing as warmly as for money: "If you are able to contribute, that would mean enormously to us, but sharing it is genuinely valuable too."
- Closes with gratitude and a gentle image: "Thank you for helping me carry this one a little further. ❤️"
- Red heart ❤️, used sparingly. No em dashes. Never "gift". Not a sob story.
- Name: Track Her Case (not "Trace Her Case").
