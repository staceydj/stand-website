# Track Her Case: campaign kit

Every post points to one link: **standaction.co.za/give**. It opens the campaign page, which carries the film, Give now and the share buttons. When the link is pasted into WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn or X, the preview card shows the smiling ambassador with the headline.

**Timing.** The 16 Days of Activism against Gender-Based Violence runs from 25 November to 10 December. Launch on 25 November, post the teaser twice a week, and close with the full film on 10 December.

## YouTube Shorts and TikTok

Upload the 9x16 film.

- **Title:** Two women asked for help. The system sent them home. #TrackHerCase
- **Description:** start with the link: `Give now: https://standaction.co.za/give`, then the Instagram caption.
- **YouTube:** pin a comment with the link. **TikTok:** put the link in the profile bio.

## Good practice

- Post the film itself on each platform rather than a link to YouTube. Platforms show native video to far more people.
- Put the link in the first line of any long caption, so it is visible before "more".
- Reply to comments in the first hour. It lifts reach on every platform.
- Ask your board, partners and ambassadors to share on the same day.
