Privacy and terms

How we handle information.

STAND treats personal information with the same care and accountability we bring to our programmes.

Your privacy

STAND complies with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). We collect only the information you choose to give us, such as your name, email address and EFT reference when you make an enquiry, a donation or a request for a Section 18A certificate. We use it to respond to you, to issue receipts and certificates, and to keep you informed where you have asked us to. We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties, except where the law requires it. We keep it only for as long as it is needed for these purposes or for our legal and audit obligations.

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. Our Information Officer is Stacey Doorly-Jones, Founder and Chief Executive, contactable at stacey@standaction.co.za.

Donations

Donations made through SnapScan or other online giving platforms are processed securely by those platforms under their own privacy and security policies. STAND never receives or stores your card details. EFT donations are received directly into STAND's registered bank account.

Figures and data

The figures on this site are drawn from STAND's programme records, donor reports and published research. They are reported conservatively and are accurate as of October 2026. Where a figure combines several programmes, people counted in more than one programme are counted once.

Photographs and consent

Photographs are used with the permission of those pictured. No person on this site is identified as a survivor or service user. If you appear in an image and would like it removed, please contact us and we will act promptly.

Publications and copyright

Reports and publications by partner organisations, including Harm Reduction International, remain the property of their publishers and are referenced with acknowledgement. All other content on this site, including text, design and the STAND name and logo, is © 2026 Social Transformation Action Defined NPC. All rights reserved. Content may be quoted for non-commercial purposes with clear attribution to STAND.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Its fonts are served from this site, so no third party receives your details when you visit.
